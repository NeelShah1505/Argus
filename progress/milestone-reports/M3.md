## 1. Deliverables Completed

1. **Deterministic Validation Subsystem (`packages/validate/`)**
   - Subtotal footing & cross-footing (`validate_subtotal_footing`): Sums lines items within configured decimal tolerance. Failures record discrepancies without mutating numbers.
   - Accounting identities (`validate_balance_sheet_identity`): Enforces $\text{Assets} = \text{Liabilities} + \text{Equity}$.
   - Sign sanity (`validate_sign_sanity`): Flags negative revenues and cash balances.
   - Anomaly engine (`packages/validate/anomalies.py`): Detects YoY swings $> 40\%$ (`check_yoy_outlier`).

2. **Accuracy Tier Enforcement (`packages/validate/tiers.py`)**
   - Implements the Evidence Contract:
     - $V$ (Verified): Dual extraction consensus + all L2 validations passed + valid bounding box coordinates.
     - $E$ (Extracted): Single-engine confident extraction.
     - $M$ (Estimated): Chart geometric measurement.
     - $R$ (Needs Review): Validation failure, ensemble disagreement, or missing evidence. Never auto-fixed.

3. **Human Review Correction Workflow (`apps/api/routers/documents.py`)**
   - `PATCH /api/v1/documents/elements/{cell_id}`: Allows human reviewer to update raw text, re-canonicalizes value, assigns Tier $V$, increments document version, and logs an append-only `AuditEvent`.
   - `GET /api/v1/documents/{doc_id}/validation`: Surfaces all validation results and audit events.
